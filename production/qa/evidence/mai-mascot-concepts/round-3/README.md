# Mai mascot concepts, round 3 — owner, please pick

Owner after round 2: "I like G for original, but still not there yet. Make it original but also resemble nail art.
Like Duolingo: when they mention Duolingo, people know the owl right away."

Goal: an **iconic** mascot: one simple silhouette, one signature colour (**coral-hot pink #FF5A87** on all four),
and instantly "nail art". All four start from G (the original mochi cat-bunny species). G's problems are fixed: no
face-mask apron, floppy bunny ears you can see, and a nail signature in the shape itself.

Each sheet adds two checks: a solid **silhouette** at 56 and 32 px (is it recognisable as a black shape?), and a
**one-colour logo** (app icon at 96 and 56 px, plus a plain one-colour mark). The "do Mai's nails" row shows French
tip, pink-lilac gradient, and mint with dots (on G1/G2 that is her whole body's polish). Original designs; no
likeness to existing characters intended. Concept sketches, not final art.
