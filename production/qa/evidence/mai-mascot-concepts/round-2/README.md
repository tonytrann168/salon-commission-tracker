# Mai mascot concepts, round 2 — owner, please pick

Owner after round 1: "Add more designs, but for now I'm leaning C." Then: "We want cute, personal, easy to customize,
easy to buy accessories and dresses... cute like the kitty works, but too common."

Each sheet: six expressions, the 56 px bubble, the bubble with a badge, the 320 px chat strip, the 160 px panel, a
sample accessory, all six accessory slots at once, **"do Mai's nails"** (three polish colours on her own painted ear
and claw tips: pink, mint, gold glitter; a new `nails` slot, the most personal and on-theme customization, and the
natural first shop category later), and a **score line** on the owner's four criteria: cute at 56 px · ownable
(not just a cat) · dress-up friendly · nail-salon identity. Original designs; no resemblance to branded characters
intended. Concept sketches, not final art.

All C variants fix round 1's weak spot: a strong dark outline, a darker inner shade, bigger high-contrast eyes and a
fuller face, so they read at 56 px.

Dev page: `npm run dev:local` in `src/`, then http://localhost:5173/mascot-concepts.html?round=2. To use a pick in
the game: set `"skin"` in `src/game/config/mascot.json` to its id (e.g. `f-polish-kitty`).
