# Story 014: Companion contests & social visits (v2, NOT SCHEDULED)

> **Epic**: Salon Besties (later)
> **Status**: Not scheduled (design note only; needs accounts, a backend and moderation)
> **Type**: Design only
> **Last Updated**: 2026-11-10

## The idea (owner, 2026-11-10)

The companion (Mai by default; female or male; renamable) is like a pet the player raises and dresses up. Later:
- **Contests**: players enter their dressed-up companion (or a nail design) in themed contests, and other players vote.
- **Social**: each player has their own character with the companion alongside them; the companion can **visit other
  players' salons** as their representative (leave a greeting, a gift, a compliment).

## Why it is not scheduled

v1 is a single-player phone web app with everything stored on the device. All of this needs:
- **Player accounts** (sign-in, recovery, privacy, age rules), a **backend** (profiles, companions, votes, visits) and
  hosting costs.
- **Moderation**: player-chosen companion names and any player-written text can be offensive, so names, messages and
  images need filters, reporting and a human review path.

## Open questions for the owner

1. Accounts: which sign-in (phone, email, Google/Apple)? Is a nickname shown publicly, or only the companion's name?
2. Anti-abuse: vote rings and bots in contests; spam visits; rate limits; blocking and reporting players.
3. Name moderation: is the v1 blocklist enough, or do public names need review before others see them?
4. Free vs cosmetic: contest entry and visits stay free; only cosmetics (outfits, accessories, salon decor) are ever
   for sale. **Never pay-to-win**: purchases never affect votes, scores or English help.
5. Safety: no free text between strangers at first (preset greetings and stickers only)?
6. Where does it live: the same app, or a separate "community" area that can be switched off?

## Out of scope for v1

Everything above. v1 keeps the companion single-player: female/male, rename, dress-up and free accessories
(story 013).
